Our First Year

The moments that became us

Not every beautiful story begins with a grand event. Sometimes it begins with ordinary nights that slowly become impossible to forget.

Chapter 01

The Nights That Started Everything 🌙

I was working night shifts while you were completing your final semester. We had studied the same course, but you were six months behind me, so our conversations often wandered between schoolwork, advice, laughter and life.

Night after night, we talked. What began as ordinary conversations slowly became the part of my shifts that I looked forward to most.

Chapter 02

The First Time Our Hands Met 🤍

You travelled from Machakos, and I waited for you at the stage in town. From there, we boarded a matatu towards Islii, where you were staying with a relative and where I was also working at the time.

You became nauseous during the journey. Somewhere inside that ordinary matatu, our hands met for the first time. The world outside carried on normally, but to me, something quietly unforgettable had happened.

Chapter 03

Nairobi Gave Us Rain 🌧️

We met at KICC and spent precious time together. Nairobi gave that day a rainy sky, but the rain only made the memory feel more cinematic.

It became one of those moments that stays alive in small details: the place, the weather and the happiness of simply being together.

Chapter 04

The Hug I Wanted to Pause 🫂

You visited me at my workplace at Bilal Hospital, and that visit gave us our first hug.

It was a simple moment, but I wanted time to slow down. For those few seconds, I wished the world would pause and let me remain there just a little longer.

Chapter 05

Until Morning 🌙

One night, I was working another night shift while you were going through a difficult evening. I did not want you to feel alone, so we stayed together through a video call.

We talked for hours until morning arrived. That night reminded me that love is sometimes simply choosing to stay, listen and be present when someone needs companionship most.

And somehow...

A whole year happened ❤️
